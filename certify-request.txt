package: cortico-world-qq-final
reason: QQ (OneBot v11 / NapCat) world adapter for Cortico. Audited on a real instance (Coopanion v0.3.3): installed from the market, connects to NapCat over OneBot v11 reverse WebSocket, receives and sends group/private messages, and all 37 tools are declared in describe.json. Passes "pnpm check:extension" (manifest, entry, world shape, browser bundle, defaults/create/tools/envPromptVars/webUi, self-description) with no failures. Ships an illustrated setup guide in docs/guide.md. Fixed four correctness bugs found in real use: an empty watch-list was parsed as QQ id 0 so it could never be cleared; the roster panel rebuilt its form after saving and appeared to revert the users input; two mismatched emoji-ID schemes made received faces display the wrong name; and the extension root path mis-resolved under non-ASCII paths. CHANGELOG.md documents every release.
